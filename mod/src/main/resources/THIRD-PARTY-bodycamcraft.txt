BodycamCraft bundles two small native decoders, used only to read the files of the Bodycam copy installed on this PC.
No Bodycam content is included in this mod.

natives/bcoodle.dll
  A C wrapper around the oozextract crate (Kraken decompression), version 0.5.5.
  License: MIT. Source: https://github.com/lvlvllvlvllvlvl/oozextract
